# STAGE 1 — THE TOWER: Credits

All 3D models in this game are real, free models under **CC0** or **CC-BY** licences,
downloaded from **Poly Pizza** (https://poly.pizza), which hosts Kenney, Quaternius
and Poly by Google models with their licences shown per model — except the auto
rickshaw, which comes from **3DAssets.dev** (CC0). Every model was optimised with
gltf-transform (meshopt + WebP textures ≤1024 px). Raw downloads are in `_source/`
(not shipped); only the optimised files in `assets/` are in the game.

- **Three.js 0.180.0** — MIT licence — https://threejs.org (vendored in `vendor/`,

## Notes
- The "girl" (player) and "woman" (NPC) models are two different Quaternius
  "Animated Woman" models with different rigs.
- No AI-generated, ripped, or unattributed models are used. No real brands,
  logos, or copyrighted characters appear in the game.
